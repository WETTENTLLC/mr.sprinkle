MATERIALS

10K vs 14K Gold Grillz

The karat number describes the proportion of gold in a gold alloy. 10K is 10 parts gold out of 24 (about 41.7%); 14K is 14 parts out of 24 (about 58.3%). The balance is made up of other metals, and the exact alloy matters.

Mr. Sprinkle's published catalog lists 10K gold at $240 per tooth, 14K gold at $300 per tooth, and 14K dental gold at $320 per tooth. Ask the team for the specific material details for the option you are considering; the karat label alone does not describe every alloy property.

CARE INFORMATION

Care Questions to Ask About Your Grillz

Care instructions can depend on the alloy, finish, stones, and construction of a particular piece. Follow the product-specific instructions supplied with your order instead of assuming one cleaning method is right for every design.

Before using a cleaner, polishing product, soaking solution, or ultrasonic device, ask whether it is suitable for your exact materials and stone settings. For oral hygiene, fit concerns, irritation, or other health questions, consult a dental professional.

Mr. Sprinkle does not describe cosmetic grillz as dental treatment or make universal guarantees that every material is tarnish-proof or suitable for every wearer.

MATERIALS & FIT

Questions to Ask Before Ordering Grillz

Ask which metal or alloy is being quoted, whether the selected finish or stones change the price, what fitting or mold process applies, and which care instructions are specific to the finished piece.

Confirm whether your design is covered by the published catalog or needs a custom quote. A cosmetic grill is jewelry, not dental treatment; speak with a dental professional about oral health, fit, or discomfort.

The published catalog does not promise that every alloy, setting, or design is appropriate for every person. Have material and fit questions answered before confirming an order.

DESIGN IDEAS

Custom Grillz Design Options

Start with the exact teeth you want to cover. The designer supports individual upper and lower tooth selection, so you can plan a single-tooth accent or a larger combination rather than choosing a mandatory preset layout.

Other catalog options include fangs, gap fillers, custom cuts, natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds, VVS moissanite, and cubic zirconia. Availability and price depend on the selected material and design.

Custom cuts are charged per set, not per tooth: Diamond/Dust $30, Center/Split $20, Open Face/Window $40, and Deep/Bar $30. An upper piece and a lower piece count as separate sets.

ORDER PROCESS

What to Expect When Requesting Custom Grillz

Begin with the design: choose the teeth, material, stones, and catalog cuts you want to discuss. The designer provides an estimate for listed options; materials or finishes outside the catalog require a custom quote.

Submit an appointment or quote request so the team can confirm the applicable mold process, fitting arrangements, availability, shipping options, and production timing for your order.

Production is generally estimated at 2–7 business days. Overall fulfillment may take around two weeks or longer after production and shipping are considered. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed completion dates; confirm the current timing with the team.
