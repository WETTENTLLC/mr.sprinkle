CARE GUIDE

How to Clean and Care for Your Gold Grillz

📅 January 2025 • ⏱️ 4 min read

Gold alloys and finishes can respond differently to wear and care. No material is represented here as tarnish-proof; follow the product-specific care instructions and consult a dental professional about oral health questions.

Daily Care:
1. Rinse with warm water after eating
2. Brush gently with soft toothbrush
3. Store in protective case when not wearing
4. Avoid harsh chemicals and chlorine

Deep Cleaning (Weekly):
• Soak in denture cleaner 15 minutes
• Use jewelry cleaning solution
• Professional ultrasonic cleaning available
• Polish with microfiber cloth

SAFETY

Understanding Gold Karats and Grillz Safety

📅 January 2025 • ⏱️ 5 min read

Gold karat describes the proportion of gold in an alloy; it does not by itself establish that a finished grill is medical-grade or suitable for every wearer. Mr. Sprinkle does not make a universal no-tarnish or safe-to-eat, drink, or smoke guarantee. Ask for the alloy details and consult a dental professional about wearing a cosmetic oral accessory.

Questions to ask about materials and fit:
• Ask for the specific alloy composition and material details
• Confirm the care instructions for the selected finish
• Check fit and comfort with the provider before use
• Do not assume cosmetic jewelry is a dental or medical device

What to Avoid:
❌ Cheap plated grillz (flake & tarnish)
❌ Unknown metal compositions
❌ Non-dentist fitted pieces
❌ Toxic adhesives
