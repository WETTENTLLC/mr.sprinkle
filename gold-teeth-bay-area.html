Custom Grillz Pricing and Design Guidance

Use the published tiers below as the approved base prices for the listed metals and set sizes. Individual upper and lower teeth can be selected in the configurator. Published custom cuts are $30, $20, $40, and $30 per set, depending on the selected cut. Stone-setting labor is included in published pricing. Custom finishes and designs outside the catalog require a quote.

Shipping and Production Estimates

Approved standard shipping is $60 by FedEx. Production generally takes 2–7 business days; transit is approximately 2–3 days to the main office and approximately 2–3 days back to the customer. Plan for around two weeks or longer overall; this is a qualified estimate, not a guaranteed date. Confirm Bay Area shipping coverage, capacity, and timing before ordering.
