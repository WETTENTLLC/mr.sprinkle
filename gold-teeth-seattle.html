Custom Grillz for Seattle Customers

Seattle-area customers researching custom grillz can use this guide to review published base pricing, fitting questions, and production estimates. It is not a Seattle office or appointment location; all physical appointments take place in Reno.

Starting a Custom Grillz Request from Seattle

Review the approved metal and set-size prices below, then use the configurator to outline your selected teeth and design. A custom quote is required for work without an approved fixed price, including setting stones and design-specific labor. Confirm service and delivery availability for your order before checkout or payment.

Shipping and Production Estimates

Approved standard shipping is $60 by FedEx. Production generally takes 2–7 business days, with approximately 2–3 days to the main office and approximately 2–3 days back to the customer. Plan for around two weeks or longer overall; this is a qualified estimate, not a guaranteed date. Confirm Seattle shipping coverage, production capacity, and carrier timing before ordering.
