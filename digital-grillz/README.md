# Mr. Sprinkle Digital Grillz Collection

This folder is the source-of-truth foundation for the digital grillz catalog.

## Current state
- The public Avatar Studio creates stylized character previews and exports a design-spec JSON file or avatar PNG.
- The existing Three.js smile renderer is reused for a live product-detail preview.
- The exported JSON is a design brief, not a game-ready asset.
- No item is currently installable in GTA VI or another commercial game.

## Future authorized integration checklist
1. Confirm an official creator program, licensed partner route, or other documented authorization.
2. Obtain the target platform's current asset, rigging, attachment, texture, polygon, LOD, and submission specifications.
3. Create editable 3D master geometry for each approved design.
4. Export and validate in the exact target formats and scale specified by the platform.
5. Complete rights, branding, and customer license review before distribution.

## Guardrails
- Do not use Rockstar or Grand Theft Auto marks in a way that suggests affiliation or approval.
- Do not advertise these previews as GTA VI-compatible.
- Do not distribute game mods or instruct customers to bypass platform protections.
- Physical product pricing, metal claims, stone availability, fit, and final fabrication are quoted separately through Mr. Sprinkle.
