Made for your nails. Not a standard size.

Every finger is different. That's why the fitting is personal: we take an impression of each nail you choose, then use those individual impressions to prepare your custom pieces.

1 Choose your nails & gold

Select any combination of fingers and choose 10K or 14K gold. The page calculates a base estimate using the current single-tooth gold rate for each nail.

2 Come in for your impressions

At your fitting, we take a separate impression of every selected natural nail so each piece is shaped for that finger.

3 Approve the final design & quote

We review your fit, finish and any custom details with you. Once the final quote is approved, we'll send a secure payment link before production begins.

How the base estimate works: each gold nail uses the same base per-piece rate as one gold tooth in the current Mr. Sprinkle pricing catalog. 10K and 14K gold have different per-nail rates. Stones, enamel artwork, engraving and other special details are reviewed separately and may change the final quote.
