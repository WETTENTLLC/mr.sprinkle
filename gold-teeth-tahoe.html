Planning a Custom Grillz Order from Lake Tahoe

Start with the published silver and gold tiers below, then use the configurator to describe tooth selection and design ideas. Published custom cuts are charged per set: Diamond/Dust $30, Center/Split $20, Open Face/Window $40, and Deep/Bar $30. Stone-setting labor is included in published pricing. Custom finishes and designs outside the catalog may need a personalized quote. Confirm fitting and shipping arrangements before placing an order; service availability outside Reno is not assumed.

Shipping and Production Estimates

Approved standard shipping is $60 by FedEx. Production generally takes 2–7 business days, with approximately 2–3 days to the main office and approximately 2–3 days back to the customer. Plan for around two weeks or longer overall; this is a qualified estimate, not a guaranteed date. Confirm destination coverage, capacity, and timing before ordering.
